handframe

Hold up both hands to make a frame. Whatever is inside it gets the filter.

Source on GitHub

Start with your camera, or watch the demo

The camera image is processed in this browser tab. Nothing is uploaded or stored.

Hand controls

Make the frame
Show both hands. Your thumb tips and index fingertips are its four corners.
Next filter
Touch your middle finger to your thumb on either hand.
Take a photo
Touch your ring finger to your thumb.

Keyboard: → ← or Space change filter, M changes mode, S takes a photo, H shows or hides hand points.

Hand tracking by MediaPipe, running on your device. Idea inspired by a hand-tracking Python demo by sy4hdan on Instagram; this is a separate browser implementation.
